# What Is It?

<br/>

:::tip What is the CAD?
The Kelly County CAD is a web-based platform that allows you to create and manage your characters, vehicles, and much more!
The Cad Also Allows Everyone to look at their, characters and what they have registered on the character, like licenses, cars, guns and more!:::

<br/>

<br/>

### Accessing the CAD

You can access the CAD by heading over to [cad.kcdojrp.com](https://cad.kcdojrp.com/). You will then be prompted to login, like so.

<br/>

![Image of Login Page](/imgs/kcrp-cad-login.png)

<br/>

:::warning Don't Have An Account?
If you don't have an account already, hit the "Login via Discord" button and you will be redirected to the Discord login page. Once you have logged in, you'll be prompted to setup a password for your account, once you choose that password, we reccomend writing it down to be able to remember it. Sometimes when you do not access the cad for a long time it will log you out of the cad, so writing down your username and password of the account will be much more time efficient

Your username will be the same as your Discord username.
:::
